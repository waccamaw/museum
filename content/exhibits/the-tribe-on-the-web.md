**2004 — waccamaw.us.** The tribe's first site: a hand-coded frameset — *"Waccamaw
Indian People Tribal Website"* — Home, News Letters, Forums, About, Contact. Built the
year before state recognition.
→ [Browse the 2004 site](/sites/waccamaw-us-2004/)

**2005–2011 — "Walk easy on Mother Earth."** The rebuilt welcome page: the Waccamaw
River at Conway, an ENTER button between two ceremonial staffs, and the words the
site became known by.
→ [Browse the 2008 site](/sites/waccamaw-us-2008/)

**2009–2014 — the WordPress years.** waccamawindians.us: the tribe's first
content-managed site — black background, dreamcatcher logo, red-bordered sidebar.
Rebuilt here from the original theme files recovered from the tribe's 2012 backup.
It was later compromised and taken down.
→ [Browse the 2011 site](/sites/waccamawindians-us-2011/)

**2013–2025 — waccamaw.org, won by arbitration.** The tribe fought for and won its
.org domain, then rebuilt on Wix through several designs before today's site.
→ [2013](/sites/waccamaw-org-2013/) · [2016](/sites/waccamaw-org-2016/) ·
[2020](/sites/waccamaw-org-2020/) · [2023](/sites/waccamaw-org-2023/) ·
[waccamaw.org today](https://waccamaw.org/)

Each snapshot was recovered from the Internet Archive and the tribe's own backups,
repaired only enough to browse — original voice and aesthetic intact.
