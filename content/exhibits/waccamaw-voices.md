In March 2021, students from Coastal Carolina University's *"Waccamaw Indian People:
Past, Present, Future"* project sat down with three members of the tribe and recorded
them. Those recordings are among the most valuable objects in this museum — not
documents *about* the People, but the People, speaking.

The museum preserves the audio and full transcripts in the tribal archive; the
recordings below play from the project's public collection.

## Chief Harold "Buster" Hatcher

Forty minutes: the outsider childhood, the trip to Cherokee that told him who he was,
Vietnam, the founding of the tribe, and what recognition means. Excerpts thread
through the halls of this museum — start with [In His Own Words](/exhibits/in-his-own-words/).
