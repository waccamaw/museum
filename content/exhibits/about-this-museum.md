The exhibits here are drawn from a **verified archive of the tribe's primary
records** — constitutions, resolutions, council decisions, oral histories — assembled
from the original scans, documents, and recordings by the tribe's web team.

- **Every record traces to its source.** Resolutions are OCR'd from the original
  scans, operative text extracted verbatim, and the source preserved and hash-anchored
  so any record can be re-checked against the original — including the handwritten
  strike-throughs and margin corrections, which turn out to carry real history.
- **Everything is independently re-verified**, and **every exhibit cites its
  documents**, so any claim can be traced to the paper — or the tape — it stands on.
- **What isn't here:** member records, private correspondence, and matters the tribe
  keeps to itself. The full archive remains with the tribe under its own rules; these
  exhibits are its cleared, public surface — and as more records are cleared, the
  museum grows.

*The exhibits are curated from the records, not authored as the Tribe's official
history — that telling belongs to the Council, the Elders, and the storyteller.*
