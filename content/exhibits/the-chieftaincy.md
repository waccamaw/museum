Nobody kept a master list of the Waccamaw's chiefs. But the tribe kept something
better: **its resolutions** — and every executed resolution carries a signature block.
Read enough of them in date order and the succession reconstructs itself.

| Period | Chief |
|---|---|
| …–2006 | **Harold "Buster" Hatcher** |
| 2006–2007 | **Hartland "Tubby" West** |
| 2007–2008 | **James Webb** *(acting)* |
| 2008 | **Susan Hayes-Hatcher** |
| 2009–2026 | **Harold "Buster" Hatcher** *(restored)* |
| 2026– | **Cheryl Sievers-Cail** |

Two things the signatures taught us that memory had smoothed over:

**The chieftaincy was never one unbroken run.** Chief Hatcher's "thirty years" is true in
aggregate — but 2006–2008 was a period of rapid transitions, with three different
hands signing as Chief in two years before his restoration in 2009. (The full chapter
lives in the tribal archive.)

**The handoff to Chief Cail was years in the making.** The December 2024 election
certification is signed *"Cheryl Cail — Chief (A)"* — acting — and an April 2026
resolution already carries her initials. When Chief Hatcher wrote in his resignation,
*"during the last more than a year, I took a step aside to allow my Vice Chief to
exercise her skills,"* the documents agree with him precisely.

And one genuinely charming artifact: **Resolution SH-08-04-2006-01**, "Retention of
Permanent Governmental Titles," grants any official who serves two terms or six years
their title *for life* — "with privilege but without responsibility and/or authority" —
and names Harold D. Hatcher specifically. Which is why, constitutionally and forever,
he signs **"Chief Retired."**
