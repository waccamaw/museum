*Photographs from the Waccamaw Indian People's own publications and website archives;
the Gene Joseph Crediford Collection, Native American Studies Center, University of

to Chief Hatcher's likeness; source credits accompany each image. To contribute a
photograph to this record, contact the tribe.*
